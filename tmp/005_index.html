January, between dog and wolf

The Cold Wake of a Childhood Dream

A boy’s longing for distant shores ends in a freezing Cornish port. Amidst the mist and the ring of heels on metal, the romantic dream meets a dead ship.

I sat by myself nursing my dream of far places among the long black lines of rock, knobbly with mussels and limpets, like arthritic fingers, running out into the booming surf.

Years passed before the dream achieved the least substance.

This happened not long before my eighteenth birthday, when a school friend and I walked through the dead of a misty night to board a ship at a wharf in Fowey, a small Cornish river port.

The berthed ship seemed as dead as an icicle. I can still hear the ring of our hesitant heels on the freezing metal deck, our whispers in the dark.
