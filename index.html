The Death of the Lone Traveler

Young seeks a way out of a rigid world. In sterile travel agencies, he finds that the wild, improvised journeys of his youth have become administrative impossibilities.

For years I dreamed of taking a much longer sea journey, but travel and war reporting intervened. Working one’s passage is difficult – impossible, perhaps – in these strict days of unions and unemployment. Passenger travel, as I was to discover, is moribund. Nevertheless, I set about finding out what would happen to someone who tried to port-hop to some far destination on the other side of the world. How could sea travel be dead?

‘It’s impossible. Even for a single gent, it’s utterly impossible.’ The middle-aged travel agent ran a finger delicately across his left eyebrow and organized a mouth that might, twenty years before, have been described as ‘rosebud’, into an apologetic smile.
